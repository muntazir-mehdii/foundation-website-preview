About us

Our story

Lanternwell Foundation was started in 2026 by Claire Whitmore after ten years as a community nurse, visiting families who were doing everything right and still falling behind.

A message from our founder

I met parents who skipped their own medicine so their children could eat, and grandparents raising three little ones on one small income. None of them asked for much. They needed someone to notice, and to stay.

That is what this foundation is for. We are small, we are local, and we answer every message ourselves. Thank you for being here.

Claire Whitmore
Founder and Executive Director

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To support children and families through hardship with practical help, health awareness and a caring community.

Vision

A community where no family faces illness, loss or poverty alone.

Values

Compassion first. Dignity for everyone. Honesty with our donors. Strength in community.

Programs & initiatives

What we do

Four programs, each built around something families told us they needed.

Advocacy

Family Voice

We help families be heard by schools, clinics and local services, and go with them to the meetings that matter.

Awareness

Healthy Start

Free talks and plain-language guides on child health, nutrition and where to find help early.

Community outreach

Neighbour Days

Monthly community days with food parcels, clothing, health checks and a friendly face.

Support

Bridge Fund

Small emergency grants for rent, medicine and school supplies when a family hits a sudden crisis.

Coming in 2027

Youth Mentoring

Pairing teenagers with trained adult mentors. Planning is under way.
